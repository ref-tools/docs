---
title: "Sharing Plans"
description: "Share plans with view or edit access via links, email, or team visibility."
---

Ref Plans offers several ways to share your work with others. Each share can grant **Can view** or **Can edit**.

## Link sharing

Set link access from the Share button in the plan header. One dropdown controls who the link admits:

- **Restricted** — Only people you've added can access the plan
- **Public — can view** — Anyone with the link can view
- **Public — can edit** — Anyone with the link can edit

New public links default to **Public — can view**. Existing public links keep their previous access — plans that were already public when sharing implied edit stay **Public — can edit**.

Anyone with a public link can read the plan without signing in. The view updates live, stays view-only, and includes a path to sign up. Public plans are not indexed by search engines.

Signed-in visitors who open a shared link get the full app experience, and the plan appears in their Recents.

Changing a public link from **can edit** to **can view** moves connected viewers into the view-only experience immediately, without kicking them out. Changing **can view** to **can edit** takes effect the next time they reload.

## Email-based access

Add specific people by email from the Share modal:

1. Click the **Share** button in the plan header
2. Enter an email address and choose **Can view** or **Can edit**
3. The recipient receives an invitation email with a link to the plan
4. They get the access you chose once they sign in

You can change a person's access later from their menu in the Share panel. Changing an invitee from **Can edit** to **Can view** takes effect the next time they reload.

## View-only access

Signed-in viewers see a live document with a **View only** badge. They cannot edit content, the title, comments, PR links, or media. Existing comments are visible read-only.

Viewers can still chat and run agents on the plan. Agent runs bill the viewer, and the agent cannot edit the plan.

Access levels are enforced server-side everywhere, including the API and MCP. The same level applies whether someone opens the plan in the browser or through an agent.

## Team visibility

For plans created under a team, you can control how the plan appears to other team members:

- **Personal** — Only you see the plan in your sidebar. Other team members won't discover it through search or browsing.
- **Shared** — The entire team can discover the plan in their sidebar and search results.

**Note:** Team visibility only controls sidebar and search discoverability. Team members who have a direct link can always access the plan regardless of the visibility setting. Their access level comes from team membership — admins and writers can edit; readers can view — and is not affected by the link setting. When someone has more than one grant, the strongest one wins.

## How to share

1. Open your plan
2. Click the **Share** button in the plan header
3. Choose your sharing method:
   - Set link access to **Restricted**, **Public — can view**, or **Public — can edit**
   - Add emails and choose **Can view** or **Can edit** for each person
   - Set **visibility** to control team discoverability
